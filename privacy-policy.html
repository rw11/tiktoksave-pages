Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

1. Overview

TikTokSave ("the App") is an Android application developed by Robert Wolański. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in with TikTok, we access the following information through the TikTok Login Kit:

  • Open ID (unique TikTok user identifier)
  • Display name
  • Profile avatar/photo

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information obtained via TikTok Login Kit is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity within the App
  • Display your TikTok profile information inside the App
  • Provide personalized app functionality

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate external servers that store your personal information. No data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok Login Kit, which is subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy available at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can remove all app data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing app data through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
